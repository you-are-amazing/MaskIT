Quick guide

Teach MaskIT a face, then use it

Enrolling lets MaskIT recognise one person across your group photos. Here is how it works:

  1. 1
    Enrol the personType a label, then use Live webcam or Upload sample. Use a clear, front-facing photo with only that one person in it.
  2. 2
    Mark them as selectedTheir card appears under Face recognition memory. Click it until it shows a green Selected tag.
  3. 3
    Choose “Everyone except selected”Under Detection target scope, pick it. Everyone else gets masked and the selected person stays visible.
  4. 4
    Press AnonymizeAdd your group photo and press Anonymize active image.

Capture your face

Centre your face in the oval, then press Capture.
